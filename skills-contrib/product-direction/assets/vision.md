# Vision

This page says where [project] is going. It has two parts:

1. **Values.** How [project] is designed and built. They do not change.
2. **Durable themes.** The broad parts of the product the maintainers invest in, across many releases.

Nothing here is about current work. [Project intent](./project-intent.md) says who [project] is for and what not to assume. The [steering list](./decisions/index.md#steering-list) lists requests the maintainers have already answered, with what to use instead.

## How to use this page

Use it before you judge a feature request or a pull request, and before you propose a new feature or surface. Read the [project intent](./project-intent.md) and the [steering list](./decisions/index.md#steering-list) too. Then apply the [reading rules](https://example.com/product-direction/SKILL.md#the-reading-rules) in the `product-direction` skill. They are kept only there.
