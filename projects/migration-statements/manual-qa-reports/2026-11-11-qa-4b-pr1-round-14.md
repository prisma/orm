### Wrong advice

### Unclear text

1. **"Replace that cast with your own conversion of the values there" does not say where the conversion goes, and on Postgres and SQLite the file shows no cast to replace.** On Postgres the planned call is `this.alterColumnType({ schema: 'public', table: 'user', column: 'age', type: 'int4' })`, and the conversion is a `using` option the user has to add. On SQLite it is `this.recreateTable({ …, retypedColumns: ['age'] })`, and the conversion is a `copyExpressions` entry the user has to add. On MongoDB the `castField(...)` call is visible and has to be swapped for a `dataTransform`. In each case I found the right place from the skill reference or by re-planning with `--convert` and copying the shape, and it worked. The advice's own pointer, "re-planning with --convert writes one for you to fill in", is the practical way, and the third next action does that. Naming the option (`using` on Postgres, `copyExpressions` on SQLite) would make the first route self-contained.
